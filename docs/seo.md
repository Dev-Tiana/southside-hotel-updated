## Implemented technical SEO

The project includes clean public routes, a route manifest, robots.txt, an XML sitemap, a meaningful initial HTML title and description, Open Graph title and description, a Twitter card declaration, headings, internal links, descriptive text, and descriptive placeholder alt labels on visual preview components. The admin route is disallowed in robots.txt.

A real public origin is not yet configured, so the application does not emit a guessed canonical URL or absolute sitemap origin. Configure the canonical origin before publication, then add canonical tags, absolute Open Graph URLs, and absolute sitemap entries that match the published domain. Validate the final sitemap and robots response from the public origin.

No fake reviews, ratings, awards, hotel classifications, or structured data claims are included. Add LodgingBusiness or Hotel structured data only after the visible address, contact details, room content, and public origin are confirmed. Do not add review or rating properties unless genuine review evidence and permission exist.

## Local search actions

1. Confirm the Google Business Profile owner, business name, address, telephone, website, category, hours, photos, and booking URL.
2. Keep the same business name, address wording, telephone, and email across the website, Google profile, Instagram, TikTok, and authorized directories.
3. Ask the hotel to upload approved original exterior, reception, room, facility, and location images to its profile.
4. Request genuine guest feedback through a neutral process. Never create reviews or offer misleading incentives.
5. Add accurate directions and local context only after the hotel confirms the details.
6. Connect Search Console and analytics only after authorized access and consent requirements are handled.

## Answer and generative search readiness

The public pages answer what the hotel is, where it is provisionally located, how to contact it, how a guest can begin a reservation, which room categories are public leads, which facilities still require confirmation, and which local experiences have public evidence. The copy uses clear headings and direct answers instead of unsupported superlatives. Private reservations and staff records are not exposed.

## Ninety day plan

### Days 1 to 30

Confirm the business identity, address, room catalogue, rates, policies, approved imagery, and Google Business Profile access. Configure the public origin, canonical metadata, LodgingBusiness schema where eligible, Search Console, analytics consent, email notifications, and payment test credentials. Test every booking state using labelled test data.

### Days 31 to 60

Publish approved room and facility photography, add accurate room details, verify the contact workflow, request genuine guest reviews, and maintain the Abraka guide only after checking every local fact. Improve internal links between accommodation, location, FAQs, booking, and the destination guide. Review Search Console queries and page indexing after data exists, and remove stale travel times, opening hours, or ratings if sources change.

### Days 61 to 90

Review search queries, enquiries, room detail views, booking starts, confirmed reservations, and contact submissions without sending unnecessary personal information to analytics. Improve pages that answer real guest questions. Refresh photos and rates through the dashboard, audit metadata and broken links, and document any remaining operational gaps.

No ranking, indexing, traffic, AI answer, or revenue outcome is promised. The objective is an accurate, useful, technically discoverable website.
