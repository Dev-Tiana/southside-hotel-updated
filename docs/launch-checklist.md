# SouthSide launch checklist

## Business identity

* Confirm official public business name.
* Confirm final address wording, postal details, map pin, directions, and local area description.
* Confirm public telephone and business email.
* Confirm domain ownership, DNS, HTTPS, and the canonical public origin.
* Confirm approved logo and brand assets.

## Rooms and rates

* Confirm every public room category name.
* Confirm category level sellable inventory.
* Confirm maximum occupancy and children rules.
* Confirm bed configuration and room size where published.
* Confirm amenities by category.
* Confirm current nightly rates and date specific rates.
* Confirm taxes and additional charges, if any.
* Confirm maintenance blocking process.
* Confirm that public photos are current and accurately depict the category.

## Policies and operations

* Confirm check in and check out times.
* Confirm early arrival and late departure rules.
* Confirm cancellation, refund, no show, and modification rules.
* Confirm deposit, payment deadline, and confirmation rules.
* Confirm minimum and maximum stay rules.
* Confirm payment methods.
* Confirm parking, internet, accessibility, power, water, security, dining, gym, lounge, and events details before publishing them as facts.
* Approve privacy policy, booking terms, payment policy, cancellation policy, and cookie notice with appropriate professional review.

## Photography and content rights

* Supply approved original exterior and entrance photography.
* Supply approved reception and lobby photography.
* Supply approved images for each live room category.
* Supply approved bathrooms, facilities, dining, and location images where offered.
* Record rights holder, permission contact, permitted use, credits, and approval date for every asset.
* Clear guest, child, staff, creator, photographer, music, logo, and artwork rights.
* Keep the four integrated SouthSide social frames in managed storage and retain the source links and user authorization record before publishing live pages.
* Replace or explicitly approve the seven representative generated room visuals with hotel supplied category photographs before presenting them as factual room depictions.
* Review the Abraka guide and attributable review excerpts for current accuracy and source availability before launch.

## Technical integrations

* Configure staff accounts and roles through the authorized owner workflow.
* Configure business email and test notification delivery.
* Configure Paystack test credentials and webhook, then run amount, currency, signature, failure, duplicate event, and refund tests.
* Configure public origin, canonical tags, absolute sitemap, analytics consent, and Search Console.
* Configure approved map experience if an embedded map is needed.
* Configure image storage and upload replacement workflow.
* Document backup, restore, migration, monitoring, and incident contacts.

## Final test gate

* Run typecheck, unit tests, production build, database migration, and health check.
* Test future dates, past dates, reversed dates, room quantity, date boundaries, sold out inventory, maintenance blocks, holds, cancellation, manual bookings, duplicate submissions, and concurrent booking attempts.
* Test staff authentication, unauthorized procedures, roles, audit records, and private data boundaries.
* Test mobile, keyboard, focus, labels, contrast, reduced motion, and image loading.
* Validate sitemap, robots, metadata, structured data, links, and 404 status behavior from the public origin.
* Keep live bookings and live payments disabled until every blocker above is resolved and the hotel owner signs off.
